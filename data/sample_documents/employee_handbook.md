# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

**Document type:** Internal policy  
**Applies to:** Full-time and part-time employees in India and remote roles  
**Last updated:** 3 March 2026  
**People operations:** Maya Chen

## Welcome

Northwind Labs builds software for education and civic institutions. This handbook summarizes employment basics. It is not a contract. Local labor law prevails if there is a conflict.

## Work hours and location

Core collaboration hours are **11:00–16:00 IST** on weekdays. Employees may work flexibly outside that window. The default workplace is hybrid: at least **two office days** per week for employees within 40 km of a listed office.

## Compensation and leave

Full-time employees receive **20 days of paid time off (PTO)** per calendar year, accrued monthly. Unused PTO may roll over up to **5 days**.  
Sick leave: **10 days** per year; unused sick leave does not roll over.  
Parental leave: **16 weeks** of paid leave for the primary caregiver and **4 weeks** for the secondary caregiver.

Salary is paid monthly on the last working day. Annual performance reviews occur in April.

## Benefits

- Health insurance for the employee and up to two dependents after 30 days of employment.  
- Learning stipend: **INR 25,000** per year for courses related to the role.  
- Equipment: a company laptop is issued on day one and remains company property.

## Conduct and security

Employees must not share customer data outside approved systems.  
Passwords must not be reused. Production access requires MFA.  
Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are prohibited. Reports go to people@northwind.example or anonymously via the ethics form.

## Remote work

International remote work longer than **30 consecutive days** needs written approval from People Operations. Employees remain responsible for local tax compliance.

## Offboarding

On the last working day, employees must return the laptop and access badges. Accounts are disabled within **4 hours** of the recorded end date.

## Revisions

People Operations may update this handbook. Material changes will be announced by email at least **14 days** before they take effect.
